The Process

  1. 1

    Learning

    Students learn about how payloads work in space, atmospheric conditions, and the various things to account for when designing for space.

  2. 2

    Brainstorming

    CircuitSavvy allows students to take control. They think of their ideas, and we ideate together upon them, turning them into NASA research studies.

  3. 3

    Drafting

    Students draft their proposals for the NASA TechRise Student Challenge, with full design description, schematics, and hardware specifics.

  4. 4

    Creating

    Students have the chance to launch their experiment to space if they are awarded a grant through the TechRise Challenge!
